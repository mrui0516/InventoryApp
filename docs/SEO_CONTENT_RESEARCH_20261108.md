## Authenticity and inspiration

An authentic product of its stated brand is different from a claim that its creative style is unique. Shared notes do not establish identical formula, manufacturer, authorization or performance. The TRIPS counterfeit trademark definition concerns unauthorized use of identical or essentially indistinguishable protected marks. The label “inspired” does not, by itself, resolve trademark, presentation or comparative-advertising questions. The article does not determine any individual product's legal status.

## Useful future extensions

Create further pages only when each answers a distinct buying question: gifting by recipient preference; understanding EDP/extract labels; exploring gourmand notes beyond vanilla; trying fragrances at the Amadora shop; comparisons with genuine staff observations. Avoid near-duplicate country/year pages and fictional tests. Measure Search Console non-brand queries, qualified enquiries and completed purchases when reporting access is available.
